THE PROJECT

A villa built to feel unpredictable.

Strange Day is a first person puzzle game developed during my fourth semester. I created the concept, game design, environment, 3D and 2D assets, UI, lighting and Blueprint implementation as a six month solo project.

The game is built around a local legend: once every year, the villa behaves strangely. The player must investigate its rooms, solve puzzles and find a way out.

Voice performance by LEYAH. Licensed audio from Epidemic Sound and WeLoveIndies.

ENVIRONMENT ART

The villa is the main character.

The complete villa was created as a connected interior and exterior environment. Warm lighting and stylized forms make the space inviting at first, while narrow corridors, changing rooms and unusual events gradually undermine that comfort.

EXTERIOR

One complete location.

The villa was designed as a full game environment rather than an isolated render. This project will also connect directly to the Environments section of my 3D portfolio.
